For OSHA recordkeeping, use the first day away/restricted — not the day of injury or onset — as your beginning date.
OSHA generally begins counting on the day after the injury or illness begins. Once counting begins, days are calendar days, including weekends and holidays when the employee is unable to work because of the work-related injury or illness.

OSHA permits employers to stop counting days away from work or restricted work/job transfer when the case reaches 180 days. If the actual count exceeds 180, the OSHA Log may show 180 (or 180+).

This calculator is an educational counting aid, not a determination of whether a case is recordable or how a particular case should be classified.
